# 📊 Indian Superstore Sales & Performance Analytics (Tableau)

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Indian Superstore Sales & Performance Analytics** project is an interactive Business Intelligence (BI) solution developed using **Tableau Desktop** to analyze retail sales, profitability, customer behavior, and product performance across different regions of India.

The project transforms raw retail data into meaningful visual insights through interactive dashboards, helping businesses understand sales trends, identify profitable product categories, evaluate discount strategies, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales, profit, and order performance.
* Identify top-performing and low-performing product categories.
* Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability.
* Analyze regional and state-wise sales performance.
* Understand customer purchasing patterns.
* Track sales trends over time.
* Support business decision-making through interactive dashboards.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

* **Tableau Desktop** – Data visualization and dashboard development
* **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Dataset storage and data preparation
* **Data Analytics** – Sales, profitability, and performance analysis
* **Business Intelligence** – KPI tracking and interactive reporting

## 📂 Dataset Description

The project uses the Indian Superstore dataset, which contains retail transaction details for analyzing business performance.

Key data fields include:

* Order ID
* Order Date and Ship Date
* Customer Name and Customer Segment
* Product Category and Sub-Category
* Sales and Profit
* Quantity and Discount
* State, City, and Region
* Shipping Mode

*Note: Dataset fields may vary depending on the source and version used.*

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard focuses on important business metrics such as:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity Sold
* Average Discount
* Profit Margin
* Sales and Profit Growth Trends

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis

* Monitor overall sales and order performance.
* Compare sales across product categories and sub-categories.
* Identify top-selling products.

### 2. Profitability Analysis

* Evaluate profit contribution by category and region.
* Identify profitable and loss-making products.
* Analyze the relationship between sales and profit.

### 3. Regional Performance Analysis

* Compare sales across states, cities, and regions.
* Identify high-performing and underperforming locations.
* Understand geographical sales distribution.

### 4. Discount Impact Analysis

* Examine how discounts affect sales and profitability.
* Identify products or categories with lower profit margins.
* Support better pricing and discount decisions.

### 5. Interactive Dashboard

* Apply filters and interactive selections.
* Drill down into categories, regions, and time periods.
* Explore business performance through visual charts and KPIs.

## 🚀 How to Run the Project

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Download and install Tableau Desktop or Tableau Public.
3. Open the Tableau workbook (`.twb` or `.twbx`).
4. If prompted, reconnect the workbook to the dataset.
5. Explore the dashboards using filters and interactive visualizations.

**Note:** A `.twbx` packaged workbook can include the data extracts and supporting files, making it easier to share. A `.twb` workbook may require the original data source.
